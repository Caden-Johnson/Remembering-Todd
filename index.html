Remembering Todd

Todd showed his love through the way he showed up. He was curious, capable, independent, and always ready to teach a life lesson or find a creative way to make something work. He loved the outdoors, photography, family, and the simple rhythms of everyday life.

If you have a story, a photo, a funny moment, or even a small memory that captures who he was to you, our family would be grateful to have it.

“The stories we keep are one of the ways love continues to be present.”
